Privacy policy

Pantry Match · last updated 28 October 2026

Pantry Match helps you find recipes from the ingredients you have. This page explains what happens to your ingredient list. In short: it stays on your device unless you tap "Suggest with AI".

Your ingredient list

The ingredients you add are saved only on your device, in the app's (or browser's) own storage, so they're still there next time. They are not sent anywhere when you add, remove or clear them. Removing items, tapping "Clear all" or uninstalling the app deletes them. In the app, they aren't included in Android backups or copied when you set up a new phone.

AI suggestions

Only when you tap "Suggest with AI", your ingredient list is sent to Pantry Match's small server (hosted by Cloudflare), which passes it to Google's Gemini API to get recipe ideas, then sends the ideas back to you. No name, account or location is sent. Like any request over the internet, it also carries standard connection information: your IP address and the app's browser identifier, which names the Android version and device model. Pantry Match's server runs on Cloudflare, which handles these to deliver the request. Pantry Match's own code doesn't store or log them, and Google never receives them. The paragraph below explains how the IP address is used.

Pantry Match uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's Gemini API terms, Google may use what's sent and the replies to improve its products, and human reviewers may read them after Google disconnects them from our account. Please only add ingredient names, never personal information.

Pantry Match's server does not store or log ingredient lists. To limit how often it can be used (5 requests a minute), it uses your IP address, which Cloudflare handles as the host (Cloudflare's privacy policy).

What Pantry Match doesn't do

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for advertising.
